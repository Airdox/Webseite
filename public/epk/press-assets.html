Artist profile

Direct club energy, no decorative detours.

AIRDOX stands for uncompromising techno with a clear focus on drive, tension and controlled peak moments. The set approach is built around pressure, dark-room atmosphere and a precise sense of when to hold back and when to release.

The sound sits between dark techno, industrial weight and peak-time movement. It is made for rooms where the floor wants momentum, not background music.
